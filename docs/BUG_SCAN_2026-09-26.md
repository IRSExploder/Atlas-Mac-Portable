# Bug scan, 2026-09-26

This is a read-only review of all of `src/atlas/` (about 104k lines), run by six parallel reviewers. Each one covered one area:
- engine: turn pipeline and AI I/O
- engine: war and military
- engine: economy and diplomacy
- state, sim, runtime and config
- UI
- providers, scenarios, map, testing and the CLI

**Verification bar.** Every finding was either reproduced with a script against real or sandbox worlds (**C** = confirmed), or traced unambiguously through the code (**C\*** = confirmed by trace). Findings marked **L** are likely but were not reproduced. Anything weaker was dropped. The eight most severe findings were also checked by hand against the code. Game-balance opinions are not counted as bugs unless the code contradicts its own docstring, comment or design doc.

Line numbers refer to `builder-dominance-oracle` at `b021417`.

## Severity scale

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Critical** | Destroys user data, bypasses a safety prompt, leaks hidden information to the player (breaking the game's core design), or is an exploit any AI power can use every turn. |
| **High** | Silent state corruption, lost player input, or a rule that is plainly broken in normal play. |
| **Medium** | Wrong behaviour in a real but narrower case, wasted spend, or a misleading UI. |
| **Low** | Edge cases, latent issues, cosmetic problems and cleanups. |

Counts: **12 Critical · 19 High · 17 Medium · 16 Low = 64**

Finding ids are prefixed by area:
- `T`: turn pipeline and AI I/O
- `W`: war and military
- `E`: economy and diplomacy
- `S`: state, sim and migrations
- `U`: UI
- `P`: providers, scenarios, map and CLI

## Critical (12)

### T1 · Other nations' covert acts appear in the player's turn summary · C
- `engine/turn_engine.py:2551`, `:2558` (`backed.opened.extend(quiet.opened)`), and the gambit lines at `:2123` and `:2129`. They surface through `JumpReport.significant()` (`:666-671`) and are shown at `ui/overlays.py:707`.
- A tracked nation's covert support and successful covert gambits are written as SECRET events. They are then copied as plain strings into the turn report, which the player sees with no viewer check. The same applies to `report.rejections`: tracked agents' refusals are listed verbatim.
- Repro: Veyra (tracked) returns `covert_support {"karthos":{"advisors":2}}`. The player (Aurelia) sees `Veyra: advisors for Karthos at 2`, while the event itself is `('secret', ...)`.
- Fix: tag each report line with the event it came from, and drop lines whose event is outside `visible_event_ids(..., player)`.

### T2 · The advance's stop conditions ignore visibility and print secret event text · C
- `engine/interrupt.py:107-181` (`triggers_in` has no viewer). Shown at `ui/advance.py:314`.
- A secret `treaty_signed`, `alliance_formed` or `construction` event between other powers halts the player's advance, and its text is printed as the reason.
- Repro: `treaty | an agreement was signed | Veyra and Karthos secretly agree to partition Aurelia.`
- Fix: pass the player as viewer and filter through `causality.visible_event_ids` before `add()`.

### S3 · "Why your numbers moved" prints secret causes · C
- `engine/causality.py:165-177` (`breakdown_of`), used at `turn_engine.py:1686`.
- It joins every diff on the player's nation to `event.text` with no viewer filter. A successful covert gambit against the player prints its full text. (INFO-002 checks a diff's `visibility_scope`, but no read path honours that field.)
- Repro: `'stability -3.0: -3.0 Sabotage: Aurelian agents wreck the Karthosi rail yards (success)'` is shown to the victim.
- Fix: apply the `known_events` visibility predicate for the viewer, and render hidden causes as "unexplained".

### E2 · Covert support is announced publicly the first turn it is sustained · C
- `engine/support.py:586-592` (`sustain_support`).
- Every sustained support row gets a PUBLIC narrative event ("X sustains advisors for Y") with the patron as actor, plus public relation adjustments. Covert backing is exposed from its second turn. The covert tests never run `sustain_support`.
- Repro: the enemy sees `[]` before sustain and `['Aurelia sustains advisors for Karthos.']` after it. Its regard for the patron goes from 0.0 to -1.2.
- Fix: carry the row's covert scope (SECRET, patron and client only) into the sustain event and its diffs.

### E1 · Any tracked power can free any other empire's colonies · C (hand-checked)
- `engine/decolonisation.py:582` (`holder = row["controller"] or row["owner"]`); caller at `turn_engine.py:2563-2566`.
- `decolonise()` never checks that the region belongs to the nation that asked. Its `nation_id` parameter is the id of the *new* state. The schema text in `state/diff.py:625` promises "Only ground you hold".
- Repro: Germany's staff lists British dependencies in `grant_independence`, and Anguilla becomes `m_aia`. That is up to `OBJECTIVE_LIMIT` regions a turn, with no war and no cost.
- Fix: add a `granted_by` parameter, refuse when `holder != granted_by`, and pass `nation_id` from the turn engine.

### W1 · A standing ceasefire does not stop gambit attacks · C
- `engine/gambit.py:995` (`_requirements_met` only checks `at_war`; a truce leaves `at_war=1`).
- `air_strike`, `nuclear_launch`, `sabotage` and `incite` all go through during a truce and never call `break_armistice`. The scripted air war and `resolution._filter_paused` both honour truces.
- Repro: three air strikes under an active truce take the victim's unit from 6 to 2.93. The truce stays active, with 0 casus_belli events.
- Fix: in `apply_gambit`, when an at-war kind targets a truce partner, call `armistice.break_armistice(...)` first (or refuse the order).

### S1 · Opening a save older than schema 20 wipes every pact's members and terms · C (hand-checked)
- `state/migrations.py:836` (`DROP TABLE commitment`), which runs after `state/db.py:88` sets `PRAGMA foreign_keys = ON`.
- With foreign keys on, SQLite runs an implicit `DELETE FROM` before a `DROP TABLE`, and that fires every `ON DELETE CASCADE`. Every `commitment_nation` and `commitment_term` row is deleted silently. Alliances survive with no parties and no terms.
- Repro: `commitment_nation` has 2 rows before and 0 after, while `commitment` keeps its 1 row.
- Fix: run table-rebuild migrations with `PRAGMA foreign_keys=OFF` (set outside the transaction), then `PRAGMA foreign_key_check` before commit. Take a backup before any save-invalidating migration.

### P1 · `--path ... --force` deletes the whole folder containing the file · C (hand-checked)
- `cli.py:56` (`newgame --force`), `:316` (`eve --fresh`), `:988` (`map world --force`). Each runs `shutil.rmtree(path.parent, ignore_errors=True)`.
- `atlas newgame --path C:\Users\me\Documents\old.db --force` deletes all of Documents. Run with `--path .\w.db` from the repo, it deletes the repo.
- Repro: a sibling `thesis.docx` was deleted and the command exited 0.
- Fix: delete only the db file and its `-wal`/`-shm` sidecars. Use `rmtree` only for `world_db_path(world_id)` under `worlds_root()`.

### U1 · "Stop" stops nothing, and dismissing the sheet allows a rewind mid-turn · C
- `ui/main_window.py:1080` (`_on_cancel_jump`) and `:1606` (`_do_rewind`, which has no `_jump_started` guard). `_do_fork` and `_do_export` have no guard either.
- The cancel handler only changes the status text, and `TurnEngine.advance` has no cancel check. After Escape, the top bar is live again, and Rewind runs on the GUI thread while the loop thread is mid-turn.
- Repro: 4 turns still run and are billed after Stop. A rewind during the run leaves `turn` rows 0, 1 and 4, so turns 2 and 3 are gone and the ledger is corrupted.
- Fix: give `advance()` a cancel flag it checks between turns, and set it from `_on_cancel_jump`. Early-return from rewind, fork and export while `_jump_started` is set.

### U2 · Backing out of the irreversible-order confirmation disables it for the next press · C (hand-checked)
- `ui/main_window.py:1037`; `ui/overlays.py:924` wires "Go back" straight to `close_overlay`.
- `_awaiting_confirmation` is never cleared on Go back, Escape or a click on the dim. The next Advance with a grave order skips the confirmation.
- Repro: on the second press the sheet is not shown and the jump starts.
- Fix: clear `_awaiting_confirmation` when the confirm overlay is dismissed.

### U3 · An aborted turn throws away the player's drafted orders · C
- `ui/main_window.py:1099-1101` and `:1385-1387`.
- `_on_jump_finished` and `_on_advance_finished` always call `clear_orders()` and persist an empty draft. An ABORTED world sim returns before any order is attempted.
- Repro: with an unauthenticated CLI (the app's commonest real failure), the turn stays 0 and pending orders become `[]`.
- Fix: keep the draft when `report.status is TurnStatus.ABORTED`.

### P2 · Codex answers are misread as quota, auth or rate-limit failures · C
- `providers/codex_adapter.py:359-375`.
- `classify` searches stderr for failure words, but Codex echoes the model's answer to stderr (see the verbatim capture in `tests/test_usage.py:58-68`). An answer containing "insufficient", "quota", "billing" or "authentication" becomes QUOTA_EXHAUSTED or NOT_AUTHENTICATED. Those are not retried and block the turn, so the valid answer is discarded. A parsed "impose an import quota" order is lost, and the player is told their quota is used up.
- Fix: match failure markers only on error lines (`ERROR` / `error:`), or strip the transcript part of stderr first.

### Decisions recorded (2026-09-26)

- **W9: deferred by the owner (option c).** The fix is small and known. Rounding the band by its *centre* instead of the true value closes the leak, but the bands reach model briefings, so it MISSes the active cassette. Land it at the next live re-record, together with #4's staged ceasefire sentence (`ATLAS_STAGED_REBRIEF`). The patch:

```diff
--- a/src/atlas/engine/intelligence.py
+++ b/src/atlas/engine/intelligence.py
@@ estimate_of @@
-        low=max(0.0, _round_to(centre - spread, value)),
-        high=max(0.0, _round_to(centre + spread, value)),
+        # Rounded by the *centre*, never the true figure: a step chosen from `value` is
+        # itself a reading of it -- ends that are not multiples of 5 proved the figure was
+        # under 40, whatever the network or the deception said.
+        low=max(0.0, _round_to(centre - spread, centre)),
+        high=max(0.0, _round_to(centre + spread, centre)),
@@ _round_to docstring @@
+    `reference` is what the viewer is shown (the centre of the band), never the true
+    figure: the step is part of the answer, so choosing it from the truth leaks the truth.
```

- **W3: approved by the owner.** A demilitarise term empties the zone at signing. If the garrison has no adjacent ground of its own to fall back to, the term is refused rather than disbanding the garrison (#19).
